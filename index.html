ABOUT

About Me

I’m a cybersecurity professional with a background that bridges technical support, endpoint management, security operations, governance, and cybersecurity analytics. My experience working with enterprise technologies and troubleshooting complex technical issues sparked a deeper interest in understanding not only how security problems occur, but how organizations can better identify, manage, and reduce cyber risk.

My path into cybersecurity has been intentionally multidisciplinary. I hold a Master of Engineering in Cybersecurity Policy and Compliance from George Washington University and am currently pursuing a Doctor of Engineering in Cybersecurity Analytics. Along the way, I’ve strengthened my technical foundation through hands-on security projects and industry certifications, including CompTIA Security+ and CySA+.

I’m especially interested in the intersection of technical security, governance, risk, and emerging technologies such as AI. My goal is to use both analytical and governance-focused approaches to help organizations make informed security decisions, strengthen their controls, and manage evolving cyber risks.
